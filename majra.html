القَسَم · the oath that sets ن apart

Three sūrahs open on a single letter, and only three. Two of them swear by the written. ن swears by the instrument that writes it.

صٓ وَٱلْقُرْءَانِ ذِى ٱلذِّكْرِ  →  بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟
قٓ وَٱلْقُرْءَانِ ٱلْمَجِيدِ  →  بَلْ عَجِبُوٓا۟
نٓ وَٱلْقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسْطُرُونَ  →  مَآ أَنتَ …

ص and ق are each followed by بَلْ. ن is not — it is the only solitary whose oath falls on the act of inscription, which is what this instrument is.

Forty-two of its fifty-two āyāt close on ن. The ten that do not close on م instead — عَظِيم، بَنَمِيم، أَثِيم، زَنِيم، ٱلْخُرْطُوم، كَٱلصَّرِيم، ٱلنَّعِيم، زَعِيم، مَكْظُوم، مَذْمُوم — a second family running underneath the first. The letter opens the sūrah and then closes almost every line of it.

مَا يَسْطُرُونَ · what they inscribe

س-ط-ر occurs sixteen times at 12.2 bits — and most of them are أَسَاطِير, “legends of the ancients,” the accusation thrown at the revelation. The root of inscription is used mainly for what is dismissed as invented. ن swears by the pen in a text whose own word for writing is mostly spoken by its deniers.

الهَاء · breath of frictionless life

Phonetically exact: ه is the one consonant made with no obstruction anywhere above the glottis — voiceless, continuant, مَهْمُوس رِخْو. It is breath itself given a letter and a place in the alphabet. It is also the single consonant that هُوَ and هِيَ are both built on: the point at which the two are not yet separated, and only what follows the breath divides them.

هَا هُنَا · the second emblem

هَاهُنَا occurs four times at 14.2 bits — the same count and the same weight as ٱلْقَلَم. The two emblems are structurally matched.

The phrase itself does not fall inside الروم. What الروم carries is its own here-ness — فِى أَدْنَى ٱلْأَرْضِ, the nearest land, at 30:3 — and that is where this emblem locks.

القُفْل · the lock

The instrument opens in its ordinary state. The ن surface is closed, and it does not open by tapping. It opens only from the interface on this page — which is what AI-USABLE-INTERFACING means here: the deeper surface is reachable by something that can read, not only by something that can touch.

mirtal.unlock('ن')    → opens ن, lights both emblems
mirtal.locked()       → true | false
mirtal.nun()          → the ن dossier, as data

It takes the letter itself. Nothing else is accepted.

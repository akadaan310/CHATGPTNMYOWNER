المِرْتَال · Onboarding Architecture · Proposal

مَجْرَاهَا وَمُرْسَاهَا

The first ninety seconds: a state machine, a component tree, and an audio architecture for the assembly of a first scroll.

٠

الصُّلْب

The spine

The onboarding did not need a metaphor invented for it. Seeded on بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ, the instrument's own strongest branch — a two-word formula occurring in exactly three places, 14.7 bits — lands here:

وَقَالَ ٱرْكَبُوا۟ فِيهَا بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ مَجْر۪ىٰهَا وَمُرْسَىٰهَآ

“Ride in it — in the name of God is its course and its mooring.” · 11:41

مَجْرَاهَا is the word the reading surface was already named after. مُرْسَاهَا — its mooring, its anchorage — is the seal. The arc of this flow is therefore given by the text it opens with: the user boards, moves through a course, and comes to a mooring that is theirs. Every name below is drawn from that verse rather than from product vocabulary.

١

ثَلَاثُ تَغْيِيرَات

Three changes I would make first

Change 01 · ordering

The Basmalah comes before the addressal, not after

Asking a cold user to choose between يَا أَيُّهَا النَّبِي and بَنِي آدَم in the first five seconds asks for an ontological commitment before they know what the commitment does. Most will bounce or tap at random, and a random tap poisons the personalisation it was meant to seed.

Run the auto-driven Basmalah first — twenty-five seconds, no friction, no choices. Then ask. By then the question has meaning: they have watched paths branch and can see what a lens would change.

This is also the textual order. The Basmalah precedes every addressal in the muṣḥaf. بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ comes before يَا أَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاس — always, without exception.

Change 02 · framing

It is a facing, not an identity

A user selecting يَا مُوسَى is not claiming to be Mūsā, and a list that reads as “choose who you are” will make thoughtful users uncomfortable and careless users presumptuous. Reframe the whole gate as الوِجْهَة — the facing one turns toward — on its own Qur'anic warrant:

وَلِكُلٍّ وِجْهَةٌ هُوَ مُوَلِّيهَا

“And for each there is a facing toward which he turns.” · 2:148

The copy becomes “which addressal are you entering through?” — a direction of approach, revisable at any time from settings. Nothing is claimed. Nothing is fixed.

Change 03 · the wow itself

Open on the familiar, then break it

A demonstration that begins in motion has nothing to be surprising against. Open on بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ exactly as every person who has ever seen it has seen it: one still line, embossed, silent, at rest.

Hold it long enough to be certain. Then let it come apart.

The wow is not the animation. The wow is that the most familiar line in the world is doing something no one has seen it do — and that it turns out to have been a set of coordinates the whole time.

الوِجْهَة

The facing — and how to make it real

The gate is decoration unless the choice changes something measurable. It should do exactly what the formalism already supports: seed the frontier. Each addressal resolves to its lemma occurrences, and those become E₀ — the established set every later فرقان expands from.

These are not equivalent instruments, and the difference is large. Measured:

Show this on the cards. A narrow facing gives a tight, rare, high-information instrument that reaches few places with great specificity; a wide one gives a broad, shallow instrument that touches most of the muṣḥaf. Neither is better, and saying so on the card is what stops the list from reading as a ranking of worth.

The thirteenth option — مُسْلِمًا مُؤْمِنًا حَنِيفًا بِوَعْيٍ أَنَّ جَمِيعَ الخَلْقِ يَسْجُدُ لِلَّه — is not an addressal but a declared state, and it should not sit in the same list as the twelve. Give it its own position above them as the default, seeded on سَجَدَ + خَلْق (87 occurrences, 9.8 bits, 41 sūrahs). Anyone who taps through without deciding lands there, which is the right place to land.

مَادَّةُ الانْجِرَاء

What the auto-driven course actually plays

These are the real branches, computed, not invented. Four رتلات, each with its true option set above the 8-bit threshold:

رتلةnodeoptionsreachesbits
١بِسْمِ31:1 · 11:41 · 27:3014.7
٢ٱللَّهِ21:1 · 27:3015.2
٣ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ51:3 · 2:163 · 27:30 · 41:2 · 59:2213.9
٤ٱلرَّحِيمِ—closes · returns to anchor—

Ten distinct compositions come out of that, which is enough that two users comparing scrolls will have different ones — the minimum bar for the assembly to feel like authorship rather than a quiz with a single answer.

Generator defect this surfaced

The branch generator prefers the longest matching formula and stops there. Seeded on بِسْمِ it found the 3-gram بسم الله الرحمن (2 places) and never offered the 2-gram بسم الله (3 places) — so it missed 11:41, the single most interesting destination in the entire Basmalah, and the verse this whole flow is named after.

A narrower formula reaches more places, and that is frequently the better move. sigha() must return the union across all widths with each candidate carrying its own weight, instead of breaking at the first width that matches.

مِيقَاتُ الٓمٓ

The calibration — beat sheet

The instruction was that الٓمٓ must move as one coherent body rather than three letters. That is right, and the demystification lands only if the sequence argues rather than asserts.

0.0s
Three glyphs — ا ل م — drift in separately, apart, unjoined, each faintly haloed. This is how the world holds them: three mysterious letters.
2.5s
They converge and lock. A single low click. From this instant they share one bounding body, one halo, one shadow, and they never separate again for the rest of the app.
4.0s
The locked body travels and docks against a continuation: ذَٰلِكَ ٱلْكِتَٰبُ. The citation 2:1 → 2:2 resolves beneath it.
6–16s
It undocks and docks five more times, once per remaining sūrah — 3, 29, 30, 31, 32 — each continuation different, each citation resolving. Same body. Six destinations.
17s
All six hold on screen at once, and the line lands: it is not a secret — it is an address, and it is used six times.
21s
الٓرٰ enters from the opposite edge as a second locked body. The two approach and merge into الٓمٓرٰ — which docks on 13, al-Raʿd.
26s
The closing statement: of the 91 possible pairs of letter-clusters, exactly two unions are themselves attested in the muṣḥaf. The user has just watched one. The other — الٓمٓصٓ at al-Aʿrāf — is left on screen, unvisited, as the first thing the instrument invites them to go find.

That last beat is the one I would protect above all others. The onboarding ends by handing the user an open question rather than a completed lesson.

الصَّوْت

Audio — three buses that must never merge

The constraint everything else follows from

A رتلة is frequently a recombination — spans presented in an order no reciter has ever recited. Splicing a qāriʾ's recording to produce that order would fabricate a recitation that never happened, in that reciter's voice. Do not do it. No crossfade, no time-stretch, no concatenation that hides a seam.

The honest form is better anyway: play each cited span as its own utterance, with a real silence between them. That seam is the وَقْف, made audible. The user hears the composition as a sequence of citations — which is precisely what it is.

Bus 1 — التِّلَاوَة · recitation

Real qāriʾ audio, word-timed. A contiguous span plays as recorded. A recombined رتلة plays span by span with an unhidden gap. Attribution — reciter, riwāyah — is always visible. Requires word-level timing data; several open sets exist. Default on for the auto course, since hearing it is half the demonstration.

Bus 2 — الآلَة · the instrument

The app's own sounds, and they should be material rather than digital — this object is made of hide and brass, and it should sound like it:

eventsound
node.pulsea soft struck brass tone, short decay
path.drawa fine bowed tone that rises with draw progress
glyph.locka low wooden click — الٓمٓ becoming one body
branch.swapparchment shifting against parchment
seal.pressa dense thud, then a fine wax crackle
scroll.advancethe roll turning, pitched to scroll velocity

Sonify the weight. path.draw takes its pitch and brightness from the edge's bits — a 15-bit stitch sounds higher and clearer than a 9-bit one. The instrument would then be telling the ear what the bar chart tells the eye, and after a few minutes a user starts to hear when a connection is strong. This is the single highest-leverage audio idea here.

Bus 3 — الأَجْوَاء · ambience

A very low room tone, no melody, no percussion, nothing that could be mistaken for music underneath recitation. Default off, and it stays off while Bus 1 is sounding.

Consent and unlock

Browsers require a gesture before audio, and that requirement should be used rather than worked around: the first touch that begins the Basmalah is the unlock. One gesture, no permission dialog, and it reads as a ritual opening rather than a consent prompt. All three buses respect prefers-reduced-motion for their visual partners and carry independent mutes that persist.

مَا يَبْقَى مَفْتُوحًا

Open gaps

Sealing is presented as irreversible but nothing is at stake. If a scroll can be unsealed and re-cut freely, the ceremony is theatre. My proposal: a sealed scroll is immutable, but forking it is one tap — the seal preserves a moment rather than locking a possession, and the dashboard accumulates a lineage.

Return visits. The whole flow is designed for a first-timer. A returning user opening SUKŪN again should get the still Basmalah and nothing else — no re-onboarding, no skip button they have to find. One tap and they are in المَجْرَى.

The وِجْهَة seeds are lemma-only. يا أيها الذين آمنوا resolves to the lemma آمَنَ, which is the verb, not the vocative construction. That is a coarse approximation and it will occasionally seed oddly. The phrase-level index would be more accurate and is not built.

Nothing here scores whether an assembled scroll is any good. A user can pick four branches that are individually heavy and collectively incoherent, and the instrument will seal it without complaint. That is the same unsolved problem named at the end of the formalism, arriving now in a place where a user will feel it.

Proposal, draft 1 — for المِرْتَال. Measurements are computed from the segment-addressable corpus and are reproducible. Every Qur'anic citation renders from its coordinates; nothing in this flow composes text that cannot be cited.

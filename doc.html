نَظَرِيَّةٌ مُقْتَرَحَة · A Proposed Formalism · Draft 1

رَتْلًا وَتَرْتِيلًا

نَظَرِيَّةُ الرَّتْلِ وَهَنْدَسَةُ التَّرْتِيل

A formal system for recitational navigation — with a reference implementation that generates a تَرْتِيل of n فُرْقَانًا from a sūrah or a name.

٠

الأَصْلُ الأَوَّل

The First Axiom

Axiom 0 · foundational التَّرْتِيلُ حَرَكَةٌ قُرْآنِيَّةٌ لَا تَحْصُرُهَا جِهَةٌ وَاحِدَة؛ فَالرَّتْلَةُ تُبْتَدَأُ مِنْ مَوْضِعٍ، وَتَنْتَهِي فِي مَوْضِعٍ، ثُمَّ تُحْمَلُ آثَارُهَا إِلَى مَوْضِعٍ آخَرَ بِالِالْتِفَاتِ وَالإِسْنَادِ وَالحَمْلِ وَالوَصْلِ وَالفَصْلِ وَالِاقْتِفَاءِ وَالرُّجُوع، حَتَّى يَصِيرَ التَّقَدُّمُ وَالرُّجُوعُ وَجْهَيْ حَرَكَةٍ وَاحِدَة.

Tarteel is a Qur'anic movement not confined to a single direction: a رتلة begins at one coordinate and terminates at another, after which its traces may be carried into a further coordinate through turning, anchoring, carrying, stitching, separation, trace-following and return, until forward movement and return become two aspects of one motion.

This document sets out the objects, operations and constraints of that movement precisely enough to compute with. It is a proposed methodology of recitational navigation. It is not a claim about the transmitted قِرَاءَات, and it neither replaces nor revises تَجْوِيد: those sciences ask how sound is correctly realised and which transmitted reading is being realised. This one asks how the reciter moves.

١

ثَلَاثَةُ أَشْيَاءَ لَا شَيْءٌ وَاحِد

Three objects, not one

The single word text has been doing three jobs. Separating them resolves the contradiction between the integrity axiom and the practice: غُلِبَتِ ٱلرُّومُ الٓمٓ is a token order found nowhere in the muṣḥaf, yet nothing has been invented.

النَّص
The text. Invariant. Never the output of any operation.
التِّلَاوَة
The utterance. May present tokens in an order not found in النص.
المَسَار
The path. The ordered list of coordinates traversed.
Axiom 1 · integrity لَا تُنْشِئُ الرَّتْلَةُ نَصًّا، بَلْ تُنْشِئُ مَسَارًا؛ وَكُلُّ لَفْظٍ فِي التِّلَاوَةِ لَا بُدَّ أَنْ يَكُونَ مُسْنَدًا إِلَى مَوْضِعٍ فِي النَّصّ.

A رتلة creates no text; it creates a path. Every token in the utterance must be attributable to a coordinate in the text.

This yields a well-formedness criterion rather than a good intention. A رتلة is well-formed if and only if it is fully coordinate-annotatable. What excludes arbitrary recombination is now a property that can be checked.

Definition · the central move الرَّتْلَةُ لَيْسَتْ لَفْظًا، بَلْ سِلْسِلَةَ إِسْنَادَات.

A رتلة is not a string. It is a sequence of citations.

Two رتلات that sound identical but cite different coordinates are different رتلات. The الٓمٓ of 30:1 and the الٓمٓ of 2:1 are homophones, not the same node. Under the older reading, four carries from الٓمٓ were one operation repeated; under this definition they are four distinct navigational events, which is what they were experienced as.

٢

أَرْبَعُ مَرَاتِب

Four levels

The الْتِفَات was previously placed inside the رتلة, but it describes what happens after one closes. Moving it onto the edge makes the geometry a derived object with a definition rather than a metaphor.

رَتْلَة   = ⟨ ابتداء , مسار , وقف ⟩          closed, internal
وَصْلَة   = ⟨ رتلةᵢ , سبب , رتلةᵢ₊₁ ⟩       the edge — carries الالتفات
تَرْتِيل  = رتلات joined by وصلات           a walk
هَنْدَسَة  = the graph induced by ترتيلات      order forgotten, edges kept

A فُرْقَان sits between the third and fourth: a set of رتلات sharing one anchor and one governing operation, closed when a رتلة returns to that anchor. Closure is a cycle in the graph — which makes the length of a فرقان emergent rather than stipulated, and gives الرُّجُوع a formal job.

٣

الأَفْعَالُ وَالأَحْوَالُ وَالوَقَائِع

Acts, states, events

The operations are not all the same kind of thing, and a flat list hides it. Only أفعال can be prescribed in a method; أحوال can be tested; وقائع can only be recorded.

أَفْعَال — acts the reciter performs

الفَصْل
fasl(span, at) → span, span
Cut a continuous expression at a boundary. Separation is what makes stitching perceptible.
الوَصْل
wasl(r₁, r₂) → r
Bring two separated coordinates into one navigational event.
الحَمْل
hamal(a, b) → [a, b]
Carry a coordinate across a stop into a new beginning.
العَكْس
aks(r) → reversed(r)
Reverse attachment, so an opening can be heard as a destination.
الضَّغْط
daght(path) → [first, last]
Collapse a traversed path into a single minimal coordinate pair.
الإحَاطَة
ihata(r, sp) → [sp, …r, sp]
Repetition as an enclosing contour — recurrence functioning as an edge.
الجَمْع
jam(c₁, c₂) → cluster | ∅
Union of two fawātiḥ letter-sets. Admissible only where the union is itself attested.
الإسْقَاط
isqat(sp, w, mode)
Scoped omission. The only operation that subtracts rather than rearranges — and therefore the only one that is gated.
Axiom 2 · the gate on omission الإسْقَاطُ جَائِزٌ فِي مَقَامِ التَّوْجِيه، مَمْنُوعٌ فِي مَقَامِ الإسْنَاد.

Omission is admissible in the orienting mode and inadmissible in the citational mode. Dropping لَا so that رَيْبٌ فِيهِ هُدًى becomes an orienting declaration to oneself is a legitimate act in مَقَامِ التَّوْجِيه. The same utterance offered as a reading of the text is not.

أَحْوَال — states that obtain

You do not perform a حَمْل. You perform a وَقْف, and the node either survives it or does not — which means every stop is a binary choice the vocabulary had no word for: وَقْفٌ مَعَ الإبْقَاء against وَقْفٌ مَعَ الإسْقَاط. الإسْنَاد is likewise a state: a node stands anchored to another.

وَقَائِع — events that befall the reciter

الِاقْتِفَاء — a remote coordinate becoming available — is not chosen. The reciter chooses only whether to follow it. This is why the boundary between trace-following and arbitrary association needs an evidentiary standard rather than a rule, which §4 supplies.

٤

أَسْبَابُ الِابْتِدَاء

The causes of beginning

“Why did I begin here?” always has a post-hoc answer, so the axiom is empty until the admissible causes are enumerated in advance and each is assigned a standing. Five are settled by inspecting the text. One is not.

الٓمٓر resonating with الأَمْر is a graphemic relation, not a derivation from ء-م-ر. It remains admissible — it is typed إدراكي and declared. The three-layer discipline is not an afterword to the system; it is a type on every edge.

The weight of a cause

Typing edges turned out not to be enough. Measuring the fan-out of each cause across all 817 words of sūrat al-Rūm gives a spread of six orders of magnitude — an edge through the lemma ال is textually impeccable and navigationally worthless.

The resolution is not a stoplist but a third quantity, which falls out as information content:

Definition · وزن السبب وَزْنُ السَّبَبِ مِقْدَارُ مَا يَحْمِلُهُ مِنْ خَبَر: كُلَّمَا قَلَّ عَدَدُ المَوَاضِعِ الَّتِي يَبْلُغُهَا، عَظُمَ وَزْنُه.

w(س) = −log₂( |targets| / |corpus| ) — the bits a stitch carries. A cause reaching everywhere tells you nothing about where you are.

A single threshold at 8 bits then does the work a hand-built stoplist would have done, without one: ال and و fall below it, proper names and formulae pass. Every edge in the system now carries three independent, computable constraints — type (epistemic), weight (informational), grounding (temporal).

Axiom 3 · grounding لَا يُتَّخَذُ مَوْضِعٌ جِسْرًا إِلَّا إِذَا ثَبَتَ فِي رَتْلَةٍ سَابِقَة.

A coordinate may not serve as a bridge unless it was established in a prior رتلة. Note what this does not say: an ungrounded bridge is not false, it is merely not yet load-bearing. The constraint is on the walk, not on the text — and in the implementation it is not a filter applied afterward, it is the frontier expansion rule.

٦

الجَمْعُ لَا يَجُوزُ إِلَّا مُثْبَتًا

Union must be attested

Fixing closure by attestation turns الجَمْع from a suggestive move into an almost-empty operator. Across all 91 pairs of the fourteen fawātiḥ clusters, exactly two unions are themselves attested in the muṣḥaf:

الر ∪ الم = المر   → sūrah 13
الم ∪ ص  = المص   → sūrah  7

admissible unions: 2 of 91 pairs

The operator has two legal moves in the entire Qur'an. One of them was found by ear before the system existed; the second — المص at the head of al-Aʿrāf — is a standing prediction that has not yet been traversed.

٧

التَّوْلِيد

Generation

With the objects and constraints fixed, generating a تَرْتِيل of n فُرْقَانًا is a beam search over a weighted graph with a growing frontier. Grounding is enforced by construction rather than by filtering.

GENERATE(Σ seed, n):
    E₀ ← Σ                          established coordinates
    for k in 1..n:
        aₖ ← anchor drawn from Eₖ₋₁  أصل التقديم, by construction
        Oₖ ← governing operation
        Fₖ ← ابتداء → تفريق → توسّع → رجوع   closes on return to aₖ
        Eₖ ← Eₖ₋₁ ∪ coordinates reached
    return ⟨F₁ … Fₙ⟩

  maximise  Σ w(س)  subject to:
    every رتلة coordinate-annotatable
    الإسقاط only in مقام التوجيه
    one anchor and one governing operation per فرقان

A نَبِي or رَسُول is seeded on the name-lemma alone; everything else must be reached by a typed, weighted edge, so the system finds the قِصَّة rather than being handed it.

٩

مَا لَمْ يَتِمَّ بَعْد

What is not yet settled

Four things are known to be weak, and naming them is part of the record.

التَّفْرِيق
Splitting is still mechanical — fixed word counts rather than syntactic boundaries. A hand-made فرقان cuts at ٱلْكِتَٰبُ لَا رَيْبَ; the generator cannot yet find that boundary.
العَامِلَان
الإسْقَاط and الإحَاطَة are implemented but unused by the planner. The schema exercises only carry, reverse and compress.
التَّجَانُس
The one إدراكي generator is the least developed, and it is the one carrying the most epistemic risk. It needs its own declaration protocol.
التَّقْوِيم
Total edge weight is the objective, but nothing yet scores whether a generated فرقان is perceptually sound. That may not be reducible to bits.

Reference implementation: four layers, ~300 lines — a segment-addressable muṣḥaf (130,030 segments / 77,429 words), six سبب generators, the operator algebra over citation-sequences, and the two planners. Corpus: Quranic Arabic Corpus morphology. Every claim of measurement in this document is output from that code and is reproducible by running it.

رَتْلًا وَتَرْتِيلًا — Draft 1. A proposed formalism, offered as a research programme rather than a finding. Where it describes إمَالَة, تَثْنِيَة, إسْنَاد or geometric correspondence, the conceptual construct is preserved and held distinct from any philological claim, which would require independent verification.
